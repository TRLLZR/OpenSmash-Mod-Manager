# OpenSmash compatibility patch

`training-modpack-v7.1.1.patch` modifies runtime/lifter sources from
[OpenSmash-Ultimate](https://github.com/Denkishi/ssbu-recomp) and adds tests and
documentation for Ultimate Training Modpack v7.1.1. Changes were prepared with
Codex on 2026-11-04. Existing authors retain their copyrights.

This separate source patch is licensed under [GPL-3.0](LICENSE.txt), not the
manager's MIT license. `base-hashes.json` identifies the affected source content
after UTF-8 BOM removal and CRLF-to-LF normalization. Review source conflicts
rather than forcing the patch. See [setup instructions](../../docs/MODS.md).

No game data, generated game/plugin binaries or Training Modpack files are included.
Obtain the optional mod separately from its author.

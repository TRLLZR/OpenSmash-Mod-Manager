# Branding

`installer/BrandLogo.png` is the OpenSmash Mod Manager wordmark.
`installer/BrandIcon.png` and `installer/BrandIcon.ico` are the application icon.
The artwork was generated with ChatGPT and prepared as transparent image assets.
`installer/BRAND_ASSETS.json` records their origin and SHA-256 checksums.

The artwork accompanies this project separately from the MIT-licensed code.
No exclusive copyright or registered trademark is claimed for the AI-generated
artwork. Third-party rights remain with their respective owners. The project is
an independent community tool and does not imply official affiliation.

Segoe UI and Trebuchet MS are provided by Windows; font files are not bundled.
